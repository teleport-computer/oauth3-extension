OAuth3 · browser extension

Your credential wallet for the web.

Apps you visit ask to read a site of yours. You approve once — and they get a scoped, revocable token. Never your raw cookies.

How it works

  1. Point the extension at an OAuth3 instance — the built-in default (pod.dstack.soc1024.com/oauth3) or your own, the way a Matrix client defaults to matrix.org.
  2. Add a site and the extension asks your browser, right then, for permission to read only that site's cookies, and syncs them to your instance.
  3. An app requests access; your instance answers with a scoped, revocable token — the app gets the token, never your cookies. Revoke anytime.

What it does — and doesn't

Reads only what you add

Per-site cookie access, granted at your click. Nothing is read until you approve it.

Goes to one place

Only the instance URL you configure. No analytics, no central account, no third-party sharing.

Verifiable by default

The default instance runs in a TEE whose code identity is remotely attestable — verify what handles your cookies.
